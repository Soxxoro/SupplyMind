# 🚚 SupplyMind — Supply Chain Risk Intelligence

> Predict. Explain. Prevent.

SupplyMind is a machine learning-based supply chain risk intelligence platform that predicts the probability of shipment disruption using operational, logistics, supplier, weather, inventory, and route-related factors.

The project combines an end-to-end machine learning pipeline with an interactive Streamlit dashboard to transform shipment data into actionable risk insights.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Shipment disruptions can lead to:

- Delivery delays
- Inventory shortages
- Increased logistics costs
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Operational uncertainty

Traditional monitoring often identifies problems after they occur.

SupplyMind aims to identify **potential shipment disruption risk before it happens** by analyzing multiple operational signals.

---

## 💡 Solution

SupplyMind takes shipment and operational conditions as input and predicts the probability of disruption.
